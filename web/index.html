Where the training labels come from

distant supervision

The dataset has no completeness labels. But its HTML (written by a resume builder) tags every field. An empty field is an objective fact that information is missing, so it becomes the label. The model is trained on the plain text only and never sees the tags.

Known limitations

stated, not hidden
  • Single data source. All training resumes come from one resume builder, so prose dates like "Graduated in 2025" are unfamiliar to the model.
  • Labels derived from HTML, not yet verified by human annotators (validation kit prepared).
  • Missing job title is the weakest check: rare (6%) and noisy.
  • Contact details and Projects are checked by rules: the dataset is anonymised and has almost no project sections.
  • Vagueness is approximated by "thin description" and "no quantified impact"; a true vagueness label needs human labels.
